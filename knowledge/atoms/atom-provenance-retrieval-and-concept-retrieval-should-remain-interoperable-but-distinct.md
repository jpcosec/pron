# Provenance retrieval and concept retrieval should remain interoperable but distinct

## Answer

Provenance retrieval and concept retrieval should remain interoperable but distinct. Provenance retrieval follows evidence lineage, while concept retrieval follows semantic organization and topical adjacency. Keeping both connected yet separate lets users move between meaning and evidence without confusing one question for the other.
