# Supports should connect evidence to the atoms or compositions it justifies

## Answer

The relation `supports` should connect a sample or structural support object to the atom or composition it helps justify so claims accumulate explicit backing. Support is not equivalence or authorship; modeling it separately implies one claim may have several supporting objects with different scopes and strengths.
