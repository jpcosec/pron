# The source-sample-atom-composition chain should remain legible to humans

## Answer

The source-sample-atom-composition chain should remain legible to humans. Technical traceability alone is insufficient if a reader cannot follow how a source became a sample, how a sample supported an atom, and how atoms were composed. Keeping the chain readable preserves trust, auditability, and practical debugging.
