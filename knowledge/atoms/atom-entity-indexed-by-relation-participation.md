# Entity Indexed by Relation Participation

## Answer

A Symbol's meaning is not a fixed dictionary entry but the intersection of all its relational projections across WiGames. The Symbol model stores supporting_fact_ids and supporting_wigame_ids, reflecting the idea that sign-to-symbol mapping is always partial and revisable. Meaning emerges from relational participation, not a static definition.
