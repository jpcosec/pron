# Secondary about should capture non-primary conceptual relevance without replacing about concept

## Answer

The relation `secondary_about` should capture concepts that are genuinely relevant to an atom but are not its primary semantic target, preserving conceptual nuance without weakening the centrality of `about_concept`.
