# Proposition-First Architecture

## Answer

Facts over properties. The primitive unit is the relational proposition (R a b), not object-attribute pairs. This keeps logical form explicit, prevents object/property shortcuts from becoming the hidden architecture, and aligns with the Tractarian thesis that the world is the totality of facts, not things. Facts are propositions with truth assignments.
