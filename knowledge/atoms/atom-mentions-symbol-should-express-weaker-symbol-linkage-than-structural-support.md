# Mentions symbol should express weaker symbol linkage than structural support

## Answer

The relation `mentions_symbol` should express weaker symbol linkage than `supported_by_structure`. An atom may discuss or reference a symbol without depending on that symbol as a principal support object. That implies the KB can record lightweight symbol relevance without overstating evidentiary support.
