# Historical atoms should be backfilled by value and use, not all at once

## Answer

Historical atoms should be backfilled with stronger provenance and sample links according to value, reuse, and trust needs rather than through an immediate all-at-once migration. This keeps the KB operational while focusing provenance investment where it matters most.
