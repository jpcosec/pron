# Samples should be the evidence objects between sources and atoms

## Answer

Samples should be modeled as the evidence objects that stand between source artifacts and distilled atoms, because they provide an addressable, annotable, and potentially verifiable surface for grounding claims. They are not just excerpts but support carriers within the provenance architecture.
