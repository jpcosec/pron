# The provenance graph is the relational backbone of the KB

## Answer

The relational backbone of the KB should be a provenance graph centered on the chain `Source -> Sample -> Atom -> Composition`. That graph does not replace documentary truth, but it makes evidence lineage, support paths, and derivation chains recoverable and queryable across the system.
