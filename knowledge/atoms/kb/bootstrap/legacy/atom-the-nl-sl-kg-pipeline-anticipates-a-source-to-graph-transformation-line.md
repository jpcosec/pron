# The NL-SL-KG pipeline anticipates a source-to-graph transformation line

## Answer

The NL-SL-KG pipeline anticipates an important architectural idea: source material often needs an intermediate structured representation before graph materialization becomes useful. In the new KB that intuition reappears in the source-to-sample-to-atom-to-graph pipeline, though with stronger provenance and more explicit intermediate entities.
