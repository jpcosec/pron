# KG-Trees contribute the idea of structured knowledge branching

## Answer

The KG-Trees line contributes the idea that knowledge structure should support explicit branching, organization, and traversal rather than remaining a flat collection of independent notes. In the new KB this insight should inform concept-graph and composition design without forcing a direct reuse of the old model.
