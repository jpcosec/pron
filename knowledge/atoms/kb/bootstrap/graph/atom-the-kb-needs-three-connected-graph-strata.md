# The KB needs three connected graph strata

## Answer

The KB should distinguish three connected graph strata: a provenance graph for source-sample-atom-composition lineage, a concept graph for atom meaning and taxonomy, and a projected structural graph for AST, section, symbol, DOM, or layout support. This separation keeps evidence lineage, conceptual placement, and structural anchoring explicit without collapsing them into one homogeneous graph.
