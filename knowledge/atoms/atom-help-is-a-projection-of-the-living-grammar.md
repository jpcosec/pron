# Help is a projection of the living grammar

## Answer

Discoverability derives from the knowledge itself: 'knowledge anchors' lists the living grammar (symbol, kind, motive) and per-surface --help is generated from AnchorDoc and CliCommandDoc documents, not from hardcoded strings. The help cannot drift from the grammar because both are the same documents.
