# Composition should provide depth without inflating atomic answers

## Answer

Composition should provide depth by combining bounded atoms into reports, syntheses, and other derived views. It should not make a single atom answer several questions at once or carry surrounding context that belongs in composition. That implies depth grows through explicit assembly, while each atom stays short, specific, and reviewable.
