# Graph neighbors should remain explicit adjacency inspection rather than inferred conceptual similarity

## Answer

`knowledge graph neighbors` should mean inspection of explicit incoming and outgoing graph adjacencies, not fuzzy topical similarity or embedding-style relatedness. That preserves the graph as a fact-bearing provenance surface where each neighboring relation is inspectable and attributable.
