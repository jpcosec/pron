# Unwritten knowledge belongs in atoms or materializations

## Answer

Knowledge that is not already written should be captured as atoms when it is durable and atomic, or as SLDB-managed materializations such as specs, docs, READMEs, or diagrams when it needs a larger explanatory or contractual form.
