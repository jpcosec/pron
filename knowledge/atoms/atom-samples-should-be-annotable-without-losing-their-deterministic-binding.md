# Samples should be annotable without losing their deterministic binding

## Answer

Samples should stay annotable while retaining deterministic binding to the exact source evidence they came from. Annotation adds interpretation around a sample; it should not redefine which source span the sample denotes. That separation lets commentary accumulate without breaking reproducibility or traceable retrieval.
