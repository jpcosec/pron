# MEEL Engine

## Answer

MEEL (Maquina de Estados de Evaluacion Logica) is the central engine -- a deterministic finite automaton over bit matrices. It implements the Tractatus substrate to project logical spaces where every proposition is algebraically evaluated over boolean matrices. Acts as a 'Logical Loss' layer between stochastic LLM proposals and auditable symbolic validation.
