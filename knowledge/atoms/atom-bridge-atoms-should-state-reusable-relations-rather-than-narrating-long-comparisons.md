# Bridge atoms should state reusable relations rather than narrating long comparisons

## Answer

Bridge atoms should state one reusable relation at a time so cross-project synthesis remains composable and queryable. They are not mini-essays or long side-by-side comparisons; modeling the relation directly implies the same bridge can be reused in many larger analyses.
