# Doctor separates unmodeled desk surfaces from broken tracking

## Answer

deskops doctor excludes legitimately unmodeled desk surfaces (drawer subdirs, inbox, issues, non-modeled top-level files) from the untracked-documents check and reports remaining findings as broken tracking/state, matching the pill that separates desk repair from sldb health.
