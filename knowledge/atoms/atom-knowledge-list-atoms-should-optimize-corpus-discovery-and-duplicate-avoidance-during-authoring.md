# Knowledge list atoms should optimize corpus discovery and duplicate avoidance during authoring

## Answer

`knowledge list atoms` should optimize corpus discovery and duplicate avoidance because the first authoring move is often checking whether a claim already exists. A simple stable list of `id | title` keeps browsing fast in the terminal while still supporting machine-oriented JSON output when deeper tooling is needed.
