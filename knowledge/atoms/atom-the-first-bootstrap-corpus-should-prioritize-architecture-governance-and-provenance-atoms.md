# The first bootstrap corpus should prioritize architecture governance and provenance atoms

## Answer

The first bootstrap corpus should prioritize architecture, governance, and provenance atoms because those frames determine how later domain knowledge will be captured, reviewed, and traced. They are not just another content area; they define the operating rules of the KB, implying that early focus there makes later ingestion more coherent and auditable.
