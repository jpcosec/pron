# Ontomap should curate explicit concept mappings across knowledge regions

## Answer

Ontomap should curate explicit concept mappings across knowledge regions. Those mappings are not interchangeable with flat tags or local taxonomy edges, because they record how meanings align, diverge, or translate across regions. Making them explicit enables cross-project retrieval and reasoning without pretending the regions already share one schema.
