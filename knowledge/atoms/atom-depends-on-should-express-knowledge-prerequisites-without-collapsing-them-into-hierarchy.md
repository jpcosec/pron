# Depends on should express knowledge prerequisites without collapsing them into hierarchy

## Answer

The relation `depends_on` should state that one atom, concept, or composition needs another to be understood, justified, or used. That is different from hierarchy: a prerequisite is not automatically a parent, child, or subtype. Keeping this relation separate lets the KB model learning and composition order without smuggling in taxonomic claims.
