# Queries should be able to isolate atoms by grounding maturity

## Answer

Queries should be able to isolate atoms by grounding maturity. Grounding maturity is different from topic or role because it tracks how far a claim has progressed from exploratory statement to source-linked or validated knowledge. Exposing that dimension lets review, auditing, and retrieval target atoms at the right evidentiary stage.
