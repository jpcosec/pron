# Information Energy E(R)

## Answer

A quality metric for matrix representations: E = 0.25 * (c + i + o + d) where c = meaningfulness ratio, i = true fact density, o = fact coverage, d = discriminative power (non-tautological columns). E close to 1 means a highly informative context. Used for async optimization, collision detection, and knowledge tree restructuring.
