# Grouped under should support looser conceptual clustering than strict hierarchy

## Answer

The relation `grouped_under` should support looser conceptual clustering than strict hierarchy. It differs from parent-child or subtype relations because it groups related material without asserting inheritance, containment, or necessary dependence. Keeping it loose gives the KB a navigation aid for messy regions without overclaiming structure.
