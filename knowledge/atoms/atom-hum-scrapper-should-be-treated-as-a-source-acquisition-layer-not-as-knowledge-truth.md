# Hum-scrapper should be treated as a source acquisition layer not as knowledge truth

## Answer

Hum-scrapper should be treated as a source acquisition layer. It captures external material for later structuring, rather than serving as the place where grounded knowledge truth is finalized. That implies downstream curation and support modeling still determine what the KB treats as true.
